Octave rollers

Register 0 is the gap between the textured rollers. Move the left thumb one gap toward the mouthpiece to go up an octave.

G4 → G5

Keep the front keys and move your thumb up one gap.

C♯5 → D5

Touch K5 or K7 from open C♯5 to leave your thumb in place.

Other controls

Breath sensor

Air pressure shapes volume and tone.

Bite sensor

Light pressure on the mouthpiece controls vibrato or pitch.

Ground plates

Rest the right thumb on the center plate and the left thumb on the rollers.

Pitch bend

Touch the plate above or below the right thumb to bend pitch up or down.

Glide

Touch the left-thumb glide plate for portamento.

Hold

Sustains a note or sends an assigned control message.

Octave / Interval

Adds a second voice or performs the assigned function.

Transpose

Offsets sounding pitch. Set it to zero to match the chart.

Program & setup

Program selects the sound. Setup changes instrument settings.

FX & sensor knobs

FX selects effects. The covered knobs adjust breath, glide, bend and vibrato response.

Setup

  1. Connect headphones to PHONES or an amp to LINE OUT. Use a MIDI interface for MIDI OUT.
  2. Select Setup → Fingering (Fn) → EWI (Ei).
  3. Use the neck strap and rest both thumbs in playing position.

For unexpected pitches, check transpose, bend plates, Hold and Octave. For stray notes, check touch sensitivity and note delay.
