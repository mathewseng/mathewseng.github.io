How to play Ship, Captain & Crew

Each round, everyone takes one turn in order. A turn is up to three rolls of five dice.

You need a ship (6), a captain (5) and a crew (4), set aside in that order: a 5 only stays once you have the ship, and a 4 once you have the captain. One roll can bring several, so 6, 5 and 4 together load all three. They are set aside for you, one die each; extra 6s stay in play. Each roll throws only the dice still in play.

With ship, captain and crew aboard, the other two dice are your cargo, scored as their sum (2 to 12). With rolls left you can stand, or pick one or both cargo dice to reroll; the new sum counts even if it is lower. If your three rolls run out first, your cargo is 0.

When everyone has played, the highest cargo wins the round. Tied leaders all win it; if nobody loaded cargo, nobody does. The first player moves one seat on each round. The first to the chosen number of round wins takes the match, and players who reach it in the same round share it.

Strategy

Only 54% of turns load the crew at all. Later players see the cargo to beat: the last player only has to match it, while the first has to guess, and a player who needs more than 8 gets it about one time in four. A rerolled die averages 3.5, so keep a 5 or 6 and reroll a low die beside it. Going first with one roll left, stand on 8 or more unless a die shows 3 or less; with two rolls left, stand only on two 5s or better.

Bots

Easy stands on any cargo of 6 or more, sometimes stands lower, and otherwise rerolls both dice. Normal stands on 8 or more once that matches the best cargo so far, rerolls a low die beside a 5 or 6, and otherwise rerolls both. Hard works out the exact chance of winning the round for standing and for every reroll, against the cargo already posted and how often each player still to roll would beat it.

Keys: 1 and 2 pick cargo dice, R rolls, S stands.
