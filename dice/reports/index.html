WHY RECOMMENDED?

Simple amounts, balanced growth, and a moderate jackpot. Similar curves are grouped so more shapes surface.

Scoring & search limits

Score out of 100: 25 for smooth jumps, 20 for simple amounts (|p| ≤ 10 or multiples of 5), 20 for a moderate maximum, 20 for jumps growing around twofold, and 15 for increasing payouts.

Recommended keeps the best score in each curve family: same initial payout, jackpot power-of-two band, and normalized intermediate payouts in eighths. Other rankings show individual schedules.

Search stops at 600,000 candidate nodes or about 2.5 seconds. Partial results are ranked only among the schedules explored, not the entire space. Starting losses and payout intervals are interleaved for coverage.

How the math works

Every die independently matches the chosen face with probability 1/6. For N dice and k matches:

w(N,k) = C(N,k) × 5N−k
P(k) = w(N,k) / 6N
EV = Σ w(N,k)p[k] / 6N

A fair schedule has Σ w(N,k)p[k] = 0. The final weight is always 1, so we enumerate the earlier payouts and solve p[N] = −Σk<N w(N,k)p[k]. Integer bounds prune impossible branches.

Fairness uses exact integer arithmetic, never rounded probabilities. Search bounds keep every Number operation within JavaScript’s safe integer range; the checker and EV proofs use BigInt. Percentages, charts, and style scores are display approximations.

Smoothness = 100 / (1 + σ(d) / mean(|d|)), where d is the sequence of adjacent jumps. Higher means more even jumps. Constant payouts score 100.

Steepness = 100 × Σ max(d[k],0)(k−1)/(N−1) / Σ max(d[k],0). Equal positive jumps score 50; a final-only rise scores 100. No rises score 0.

Recommended = .25 × smoothness + 20 × simple share + 20/(1 + max/50) + 20/(1 + bend) + 15 × increasing-jump share. Bend averages |log₂((|next jump|+1)/(|previous jump|+1))−1|, plus 1 for each shrinking jump. The maximum term uses max(0, maximum payout).

Winning means a positive net payout. All-match probability is always 1/6N, even if a non-increasing schedule pays its maximum elsewhere.
