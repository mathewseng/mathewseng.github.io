How to play Mia

Mia, also called Mäxchen, is a bluffing game for two to six players. Everyone starts with the same number of lives.

Two dice are read with the higher die first: a 3 and a 1 is 31, a 6 and a 4 is 64. From lowest to highest:

  1. 31, 32, 41, 42, 43, 51, 52, 53, 54, 61, 62, 63, 64, 65
  2. Doubles: 11, 22, 33, 44, 55, 66
  3. Mia (a 2 and a 1), the best roll of all

A round

The round’s first player rolls in secret and announces a value. It can be the truth or a bluff.

The next player either calls the bluff or believes it. To believe it, they take the dice, roll in secret and must announce more than the last announcement, again truth or bluff.

On a call the dice are shown. If they are lower than announced, the announcer loses a life. If they match or beat the announcement, the caller loses a life. Either way the round ends.

Mia

Nothing beats Mia, so after an announced Mia the next player must call or concede. Calling a real Mia costs 2 lives; calling a bluff costs the announcer 1. Conceding costs 1 life and the dice stay hidden.

Winning

Whoever lost a life starts the next round, or the next player still in if they are out. A player with no lives left is out, and the last player standing wins.

Bots

Easy calls or believes mostly at random and sometimes bluffs when the truth would do. Normal tells the truth when it can, bluffs with the smallest raise, and calls once fewer than about 40% of rolls would reach the announcement. Hard tells the truth when it can and bluffs low, and weighs the odds an announcement is true (the smallest raise is the classic bluff) against the risk of having to beat it, counting Mia’s 2-life stakes.

Keys: R rolls or believes and rolls, C calls, arrow keys pick a value, Enter announces it or starts the next round.
