How to play Pig

On your turn, roll one die as many times as you like. Every roll from 2 to 6 adds to your turn total.

Hold to bank the turn total and pass the die. Roll a 1 and the turn total is lost; the die passes on.

The first player to bank the target score wins.

Strategy

Each roll risks everything at stake for an average gain of 4 points, so holding at about 20 maximises your score per turn. When an opponent nears the finish, keep rolling: a safe hold no longer wins.

Bots

Easy holds around 15. Normal holds at 20. Hard plays Neller and Presser’s “keep pace and end race” rule: hold at 21 plus an eighth of the score gap, and roll for the win once anyone reaches 71.

Keys: R rolls, H holds.
