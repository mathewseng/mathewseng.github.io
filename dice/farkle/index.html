How to play Farkle

Start your turn by rolling all six dice. After every roll, set aside at least one scoring die or combination. Then bank your turn total, or roll the dice you have left to add more.

A roll with nothing that scores is a Farkle: you lose the turn total and the dice pass on.

Hot dice: once all six dice have been set aside as scoring, roll all six again and keep building the same turn total.

Combinations count only within a single roll, and every die you set aside must score. A selection scores by its best split: four 1s are three 1s and a single 1, worth 1,100.

Winning

When a player banks the target (10,000 or 5,000), everyone else gets one final turn. The highest score then wins; tied players share the win.

With an opening minimum of 500, a player who has not banked yet can only bank a turn worth 500 or more.

Bots

Easy sets aside every scoring die and banks at about 300 to 400, however many dice are left. Normal keeps sets, or a single 1 or 5 to reroll more dice, and banks sooner as the dice run out. Hard plays the move that maximises the points a turn is expected to bank, worked out over every possible roll. It presses on when a rival nears the target, keeps rolling past the target when a close rival could beat a smaller total, and plays its final turn to beat the leader.

Keys: 1 to 6 pick dice, R keeps and rolls, B banks.
