How to play Yacht

The game lasts 13 rounds. On your turn, roll all five dice. Then hold any dice you like and reroll the rest, up to three rolls in all.

After any roll you may stop and fill one open box on your scorecard; after the third roll you must. Every turn fills exactly one box, and a box takes 0 when the dice do not fit it.

BoxScores
Ones to SixesThe total of the dice showing that face
Upper bonus35 once Ones to Sixes reach 63
Three of a kindThe total of all five dice, with at least three alike
Four of a kindThe total of all five dice, with at least four alike
Full house25 for three of one face and two of another
Small straight30 for four faces in a row
Large straight40 for five faces in a row
Yacht50 for five alike
ChanceThe total of all five dice

After 13 rounds the highest total wins; tied players share the win.

Extra Yachts

Once your Yacht box holds 50, every further Yacht adds a 100 bonus and becomes a joker. It must fill its own upper box (five Fours go in Fours) if that box is open. Otherwise it may fill any open lower box, where Full house, Small straight and Large straight score their full 25, 30 and 40. If the lower boxes are full too, it puts 0 in an open upper box.

Five alike is not a full house on its own, and a 0 in the Yacht box earns no bonus and no joker.

Strategy

Three of each face makes exactly 63, so a fourth Five or Six banks spare points towards the bonus, while a poor roll is often best placed in Ones or Chance.

Bots

Easy holds its most common face or an open straight draw and takes whichever box scores most right now. Normal looks one roll ahead, weighs each box against what it usually scores, and sometimes holds the wrong dice. Hard works out the exact odds of every hold over both remaining rolls and weighs each box against its usual score and its pull towards the upper bonus; alone it averages about 240.

Keys: 1 to 5 hold dice, R rolls.
