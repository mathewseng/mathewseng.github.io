Poker Rush

Start with 5, 6 or 7 cards. Tap a card, click it, or press its number key (1–7) to discard it and draw.

Any five-card straight or better scores at once, clears from your hand and refills from the deck.

  • Straight, flush, full house: 1 point
  • Four of a kind: 3 points
  • Straight flush: 5 points
  • Royal flush: 10 points

Daily games use your local date as the seed (YYYYMMDD). Random games show their seed in the header. Multiplayer races two players on one shared deck and clock.
