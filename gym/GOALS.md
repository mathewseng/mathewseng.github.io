## Goal 3: Build Muscle

Primary objective:

- Increase overall muscular size while improving strength.

Effort guidance:

- Heavy compound exercises: usually 1–3 reps in reserve
- Machine and cable compounds: usually 1–2 reps in reserve
- Isolation exercises: usually 0–2 reps in reserve
- Back-sensitive heavy movements: usually 2–4 reps in reserve
- Safe isolation exercises may occasionally reach technical failure

Preferred repetition ranges:

- Main compounds: 5–10
- Secondary presses and rows: 8–12
- Isolation movements: 10–20
- Pull-ups: submaximal volume with occasional testing

Next action:

- Continue progressive training while recording body weight, nutrition, and exercise volume; no muscle-gain rate can be calculated from the data recorded so far.

- In progress

## Goal 4: Pick Up and Carry Girlfriend Comfortably

Target load:

- Approximately 120 lb person

Goal:

- Lift and carry her safely and confidently while making it feel easy.

Important context:

- A person is more difficult to carry than a balanced 120 lb implement.
- The load is uneven and may move.
- The task requires legs, back, arms, grip, trunk stability, and technique.

Supporting exercises:

- Squat or leg press
- Goblet squat
- Split squat
- Romanian deadlift only when back is healthy
- Trap-bar deadlift if available and pain-free
- Farmer's carry
- Front-loaded carry
- Pull-ups
- Rows
- Curls
- Core bracing

Suggested indicators:

- 10 strict pull-ups
- 145 lb Smith-machine bench
- Farmer's carry with 120–160 lb total for at least 30 seconds
- Pain-free leg strength substantially above 120 lb
- Strong front-loaded carrying capacity
- Stable bracing without back pain

Safety:

- Do not practice while the back is painful.
- Keep the person close to the torso.
- Avoid twisting.
- Use a stable stance.
- Avoid stairs and obstacles.
- Stop if pain occurs.

Next action:

- Repeat the August 10 Smith-squat and leg-press baselines while recording back pain before, during, and after training; then establish a controlled carry baseline.

Current status:

- Building foundation
