# Gym Training Log

## Conventions

- All Smith-machine bench weights include the 25 lb Smith bar.
- Smith-machine and free-weight barbell lifts are different exercise records.
- Failed attempts are recorded separately from completed repetitions.
- Unknown information remains unknown.
- Warm-ups and ramp-up sets should not automatically be treated as working sets.
- Volume is calculated only when both weight and repetitions are known.
- Per-side weights must be labeled when known.
- Undated entries remain undated. Their order below reflects the supplied narrative, not inferred calendar dates.
- Workout/readiness context descriptions remain source prose unless the user has provided a 0–6 rating.
