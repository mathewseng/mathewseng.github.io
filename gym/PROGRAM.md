# Current Training Program

## General Structure

Use a rotating Push / Pull / Legs schedule for approximately 4–5 weekly sessions.

The application should not assume fixed weekdays. Instead, suggest the next workout according to:

- Last workout type
- Days since last session
- Readiness
- Illness severity
- Travel disruption
- Soreness
- Back symptoms

Workout and readiness context is recorded with optional 0–6 integer ratings. Energy and sleep use `6 = best`; symptom/disruption ratings use `6 = worst`. Missing values remain unknown. Suggestions must explain which recorded inputs affected them.

## Easy Full-Body Session

Use after travel, during mild fatigue, or when returning after time off.

1. Lat pulldown: 2–3 × 10
2. Smith bench or incline machine: 2–3 × 8
3. Goblet squat or leg press: 2–3 × 10
4. Standing dual-cable row: 2 × 10
5. Lateral raise: 2 × 12–15
6. Optional curls or pushdowns: 2 × 10–15

Use approximately 3–4 reps in reserve. No failed repetitions.

## Readiness-Based Adjustments

The rules are deterministic guidance, not a diagnosis:

- Low energy, poor sleep, significant soreness, illness, or recent travel should reduce intensity and volume.
- Back-pain severity should remove painful or heavily back-loaded movements.
- Sharp pain is a stop signal, not a reason to simply lower the score.
- Do not recommend one-repetition maximum testing by default.
- Do not recommend repeated failed attempts.
- Allow the athlete to override a suggestion while retaining the recorded context.

### Low-Readiness Push Reference

- Bench at approximately 75–85 lb
- Two or three controlled sets
- Approximately 3–4 reps in reserve
- One or two sets per accessory
- No heavy single testing
