# Health and Training Disclaimer

This website provides general workout, nutrition-tracking, and organizational information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or individualized coaching.

Stop an exercise if it causes sharp pain.

Do not train through severe illness.

Seek qualified medical care for symptoms such as:

- Persistent or worsening back pain
- Pain radiating into an arm or leg
- Numbness
- Weakness
- Loss of coordination
- Chest pain
- Fainting
- Severe shortness of breath
- Other concerning symptoms

Supplement tracking does not imply that a dose is appropriate or safe for a specific person. Discuss prolonged high-dose supplement use, relevant medical conditions, and medication interactions with a qualified clinician.
