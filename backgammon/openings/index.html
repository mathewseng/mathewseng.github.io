About the rankings

Rankings use eXtreme Gammon’s published opening book 3.70 (2013) wherever available. These are imported XG values, not results computed by this site. Close alternatives are within 0.040 equity of best. Equity is in current-cube points from the moving player’s perspective; loss is the gap to the highest listed value.

The XG reference covers 15 openings and 714 replies, with Jacoby, beavers and raccoons on. It mixes finite rollouts and XGRoller++ evaluations. Published confidence bounds appear where available; neither the rankings nor EVs are exact. Only source-listed alternatives are compared. XG data-use terms.

All six doubles are included as starting-position studies. Every legal starting move is evaluated at 5 ply with GNUbg, matching the deep book’s search depth, with Jacoby on and beavers off. Close alternatives use the same depth. These are tree-search estimates, not XG rollouts or exact solutions. Actual opening ties reroll, so these studies have no reply branch. Replies to standard openings include all six doubles.

If the reference is unavailable or a position is unlisted, the guide uses clearly labeled GNUbg estimates with beavers off. Solver uses XG references only with matching rules; other positions and unlisted submitted moves receive a complete GNUbg comparison. The independent GNUbg accuracy experiments remain separate from this reference book.
