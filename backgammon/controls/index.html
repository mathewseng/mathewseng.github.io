Clicks & highlights

Clicks & taps ↓ Checker & point highlights ↓

What happens when I click?

Tap a disc to select it. Tap the open point area to play there.

Follow the questions from top to bottom. Only your own checkers with a legal move can be selected. During a preview or the opponent’s turn, the board does not accept moves.

  1. 1

    Did you click your checker disc?

    Yes →

    Already selected? Deselect it.

    Otherwise, if it has a legal move, select it and show its destinations.

    A blocked checker leaves your current selection unchanged. A disc click never moves or undoes it.

    No · continue ↓
  2. 2

    Can the selected checker reach this point?

    Yes →

    Play the selected checker.
    This also works onto a friendly stack. A highlighted return or alternate-die target revises the draft.

    No · continue ↓
  3. 3

    Can another checker land here?

    Yes →

    Play the nearest legal checker.
    Repeated clicks in the open point area can build a point or use doubles.

    No · continue ↓
  4. 4

    Is a movable checker on this point?

    Yes →

    Select that checker.
    If it is already selected, deselect it. A legal draft return also counts as a move.

    No · continue ↓
  5. 5

    Is this a legal draft-return target?

    Yes →

    Return the nearest eligible checker.
    Restore the dice it used. Otherwise, keep the board and current selection unchanged.

    No legal action → no move

Why is this highlighted?

Highlights show legal actions, never the engine’s preferred move.

They use complete legal turns with the unused dice, including combined moves and doubles. Bar entry, blocked points, maximum dice use and bearing off all apply. Colors follow your board theme; rings, arrows and labels also identify the action.

  1. 1

    Can you move on this board right now?

    No →

    No legal-destination highlights.
    Opponent turns, previews and non-moving phases do not offer moves. Last-turn markers and keyboard focus have separate meanings.

    Yes · continue ↓
  2. 2

    Is a checker selected?

    Yes →

    Only that checker’s routes are highlighted.
    Its disc has a solid selection ring. Other source rings disappear. Check its legal forward moves, alternate-die landings and draft returns below.

    No · use all eligible checkers ↓
  3. 3

    No selection: can this checker move or return?

    Yes →

    Show a dashed source ring.
    A checker with a legal remaining-dice move or reversible draft move is a source. Blocked checkers have no source ring. Only the bar is a forward source while entry is required.

    For each destination · continue ↓
  4. 4

    Can an eligible checker reach this location?

    Yes →

    Show a colored point wash and outline.
    With a selection, show the dice used and a landing ring where space permits. A sum means one checker uses multiple dice; ↔ changes its first die. Legal bearoff destinations light the OFF tray.

    Also check for a return ↓
  5. 5

    Does this location restore a draft move?

    Yes →

    Use the undo color and dashed outline.
    Amber in the default theme; ↶ when selected. A selected return takes visual priority over a forward route. Without selection, the forward highlight wins if both are possible. Forced steps cannot be returned.

    No forward or return route → no destination highlight

Last move: outlined checkers mark where the opponent ended; hollow ghosts show where they started. These are history, not available moves.

Keyboard focus: a rectangular outline shows which point receives keyboard input. Focus does not mean the point is legal or selected.

When there is a real choice

Equivalent routes need no prompt. A combined shortcut prefers a hitting route; different hits or dice use may open a chooser. Move through an intermediate point manually to avoid a hit.

Bar, undo & dragging

Enter from the bar first. Legal routes respect all dice and bearing-off rules. Drag to a highlighted destination; an invalid drop cancels. Select a moved checker and its dashed return target, or use Undo/Reset.

Keyboard

Point keys select or play the selected route. Press the selected point again to deselect. Rapid presses within 350 ms build a point; Shift requests destination input. Ordinary keys retain the unambiguous undo-only shortcut. Change bindings in Settings.
