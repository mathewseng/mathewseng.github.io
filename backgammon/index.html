About the rankings

Best means highest evaluated equity in the site’s GNUbg opening book. Close alternatives are within 0.040 equity of best. Equity is expected value in current-cube points, from the player making the move’s perspective; loss is the gap to best.

Cubeful money play: centered cube, Jacoby on, beavers and raccoons off. Validated Short/deep rollouts cover all 15 openings and 51 selected replies; other replies retain five-ply estimates. Replies cover all 21 rolls after every best or close opening. Rollout values show trial counts and approximate 95% sampling intervals; these exclude model and candidate-screening error. Rankings can change with match scores or further analysis. Compare against published XG results. Opening doubles are excluded because tied opening dice are rerolled.
