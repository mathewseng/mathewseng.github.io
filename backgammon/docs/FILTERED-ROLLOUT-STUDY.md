# Corrected filtered-checker rollout study

The fork's `FindnSaveBestMoves` restored all legal moves after searching its finalists, then sorted that combined list. This could replace a deeply searched finalist with a discarded candidate's shallow score. The [minimal production patch](../engine/source/filtered-rollout-bg9.patch) removes that extra sort, matching upstream GNUbg's selection behavior. It changes filtered rollout checker decisions, rather than fitting values to XG.

Historical filtered rollouts remain valid records of the policy actually executed, but cannot be interpreted as the intended normal or large-filter GNUbg policy. Their raw inputs and results remain unchanged. Direct `ScoreMove` checker evaluations, the internal-pruning audit, and direct-evaluation opening books do not use the defective mixed-depth selection at their root. Basic zero-ply rollout selection evaluates every candidate at the same depth. Live fallback no longer serves the affected historical rollout book.

The new [fixed plan](filtered-rollout-v1-inputs.json) retains all 24 candidates from opening 21 and the six remaining replies, using their original published XG references. Corrected normal 2-ply checker / 2-ply cube rollouts cover every candidate. For 44 after the 41 slot, both retained candidates also receive the identical buggy normal policy as a causal control, and corrected large-filter 3-ply checker rollouts as a strength comparison. The deeper arm changes depth and filter together.

Each candidate/policy receives 16,384 full-game trials: 512 independent blocks of 32, with shared dice within each position and block. There are 28 candidate/policy combinations, 14,336 records, and 458,752 games. All seeds are disjoint from earlier planned studies and the excluded diagnostics. The original 26-case runtime validation uses 123456789 and 987654321; the new 12-case platform fixture uses 31811004. Sixty-four trial offsets from each diagnostic seed are reserved. No study equity is inspected before the entire fixed sample completes.

All arms use the previously validated research money-game rules: Jacoby, legal beaver/raccoon offer branches, cube ceiling 1,024, and the cube-context cache correction. Future cube backups remain GNUbg approximations, and counterfactual branches share checker lookahead under the original cube context. There is no fixed-turn cutoff or late weakening. Both caches clear before each batch. One-ply variance-control depth is identical, but its values depend on each policy's trajectory. Exact two-sided bearoff remains eligible. These settings do not claim access to XG's proprietary evaluator or identical future cube policy.

Four families receive separate two-sided simultaneous 95% Student-t / Bonferroni intervals: 19 move gaps, two effects on the focus move gap, four effects on individual focus candidates, and 28 absolute EV differences. Absolute comparisons report every mean-minus-XG difference, whether its magnitude is at most 0.005, and separately whether the full simultaneous interval lies within ±0.005 around the printed XG estimate. The published XG confidence margins are retained; they are not treated as zero uncertainty or combined into a claimed joint simultaneous interval. Pairing is preserved when estimating each move gap and each policy effect.

The runtime is prepared before collection. [The builder](../scripts/build-filtered-rollout-native.mjs) produces a legacy binary and a corrected binary differing only in the final-sort patch. [Platform validation](../scripts/validate-filtered-rollout-platform.py) checks excluded cases. Reusing an existing legacy validation requires byte-identical executable and assets. The Linux ARM64 preparation workflow produces a bundle, and [the freezer](../scripts/freeze-filtered-rollout-runtime.py) checks its hashes, old-binary identity, and validations before signing the final plan. A plan with `collectionEnabled: false` cannot collect study samples.

[The runner](../scripts/run-filtered-rollout-study.mjs) uses 20 disjoint shards with four active workers per shard. Each worker validates both frozen binaries and the excluded platform fixture. Counts and timings are the only progress output. The merge requires all 20 completed shards, matching plans and runtime validation, every unique planned record, and valid telemetry before the statistical summary can be written. Incomplete shards may resume the same plan; the sample size, seeds, candidate set, and policies cannot change after collection begins.

The selected seven-position cohort diagnoses the known disagreements and the causal effect of the sorting defect. It does not establish accuracy for all openings and replies, and 16,384 trials may still leave small move gaps or the ±0.005 EV target unresolved. Corrected results will be reported whether they improve or worsen agreement with XG; no historical results will be pooled into the new estimates.
