# Published XG reference book

The user selected XG reference values as the study-book source, with explicit attribution and GNUbg fallback. `build-xg-reference-book.mjs` packages the collected book 3.70 (2013) from [XG’s opening-book page](https://www.extremegammon.com/Openingbook.aspx), which permits free use even without an XG purchase. This is data reuse, not XG code or a reimplementation of its proprietary evaluator.

The imported unlimited-money subset contains 729 decisions (15 openings and 714 replies) and 2,584 candidate values. Exact published equities, marginal confidence bounds, probabilities, method, trial counts and source links are retained. Its finite rollouts and XGRoller++ evaluations are estimates. Every candidate is matched to a legal resulting board rather than compared by notation string. The source’s inconsistent opening 6–5 XGID is normalized to the standard opening position; the original and correction remain in the collected source. The packaged file records the source SHA-256 and retrieval timestamp.

The Openings page uses source-listed moves within 0.040 equity of the highest listed value. XG’s rankings win ties by published rank. It does not fill unlisted alternatives with another engine’s values. Published marginal bounds are shown directly; they are not converted into paired move-gap confidence intervals. Numerical agreement with imported data is a fidelity check, not an accuracy experiment.

Reference rules are money play, Jacoby, beavers and raccoons on, centered cube 1. Study previews and Solver exports retain these rules. Live lookup also accepts centered money cubes raised by opening ties: multiplying the starting stake scales every outcome equally, leaving current-cube-unit equities and rankings unchanged. Ownership and Jacoby status do not change on an opening tie. Other rule contexts and owned cubes remain on GNUbg. A submitted move not listed by XG falls back as a complete GNUbg comparison under the live position’s rules, without a mixed-source grade. Missing XG assets also leave live GNUbg available. Once a reference has loaded, it supersedes a previously cached live estimate for bot/hint choices.

The study guide’s GNUbg fallback uses the existing beavers-off book and says so. A separate five-ply supplement covers all 21 replies after 5–1 24/18, a close XG opening absent from both reply books. Its reproducible native search evaluates every legal move at three plies, the top ten plus the 0.080 window at four plies, then the union of three/four-ply top two and 0.040-window moves at five plies. It preserves the original deep-book entries and provenance. This fallback has no XG reference to compare against.

`xg-reference-book.test.mjs` checks all published values and legal moves in both orientations, source hash, rule rejection, consistent grading and coverage. It also compares the live client with the guide for all standard openings and all 672 replies to its 32 close opening choices, including increased centered cubes. `bot-opening-parity.cjs` exercises real bot commits for opening 6–4 in both colors and replies to its two leading alternatives. The independent 32,768-trial six-reply study still uses the unchanged GNUbg policy and its predeclared seeds/sample. Its result cannot be inferred from this import. Independent GNUbg agreement and the 0.005 EV audit remain visible in the report.

## Source credits and rules

The [data-use terms](https://www.extremegammon.com/GetOb.aspx) require preservation and display of each original contributor. `collect-xg-attribution.py` extracts those credits from the cached HTML after verifying every page against the frozen comparison source SHA-256. The companion `xg-reference-attribution.json` preserves author, date and XG version for all 2,584 moves without changing the experiment’s source inputs. The packaged reference and displayed move lists retain these credits; the roll grid maps numbered credits to its contributor legend.

The source labels the rules “Jacoby Beaver”. XG exposes one Beaver flag; its [manual](https://www.extremegammon.com/extremegammon2.pdf) describes raccoons as a response to beavers and excludes further immediate redoubles (otters). We map that setting to this app’s beaver-and-raccoon mode (two immediate redoubles), and reject beaver-only live positions. This corrects the initial import’s unsupported “raccoons off” assumption; it does not change the frozen independent GNUbg trials, which use neither.

The imported cubeful equity is verbatim. The ancillary cubeless field is derived from the rounded published probabilities using `2W − 1 + WG + WBG − LG − LBG`, and is marked as derived in saved results. This convention agrees with XG’s published examples (for example [the 2015 final](https://www.extremegammon.com/v2/MCFinal2015/game2.htm)); it is not an additional precision claim.
