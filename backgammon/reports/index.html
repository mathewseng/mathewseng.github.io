One-second automatic search

Auto balances speed and decision stability, up to four plies within a one-second target. It can stop early for a clear, stable lead or when the next depth is unlikely to fit. Engine loading is separate.

The new gap/cost policy averaged 374 ms versus 687 ms for deadline-only search across 68 paired test cases on an M4 Pro, choosing the same move in all 68.
