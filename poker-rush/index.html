Poker Rush

Start with 5, 6, or 7 cards. Tap a card, click it, or press its number key to discard and draw.

Any 5-card straight-or-better hand scores immediately, clears from your hand, and refills from the deck.

  • Straight, flush, full house: 1 point
  • Four of a kind: 3 points
  • Straight flush: 5 points
  • Royal flush: 10 points

Daily games use your local date as the seed in YYYYMMDD format. Random games show the seed in the table header.
