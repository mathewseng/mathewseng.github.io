How to read it

EV definitions

Royalty EV is the expected maximum immediate royalty score. Deals with no legal board contribute zero.

Royalty EV
Expected immediate royalties; fouled deals score zero.
Repeat FL
Chance that at least one qualifying repeat line exists.
Foul
Chance that the deal cannot make a legal board.
Repeat-line
Average royalties on the best repeat line, only when one exists.
Recursive
Expected total royalties across future repeats. Joker games weight later hands by 0J, 1J, and 2J draw probabilities from a 54-card deck.
Deck mix
Weighted average over the possible joker counts drawn from a one- or two-joker deck.
