How to read it

EV definitions

Immediate EV is the average maximum royalty score on the current deal, without adding value from future repeats.

Immediate
Highest royalty line, whether it repeats or not.
Repeat
Chance that at least one qualifying repeat line exists.
Repeat-line
Average royalties on the best repeat line, only when one exists.
Recursive
Expected total royalties across future repeats, with finite-sample smoothing on the repeat rate.
