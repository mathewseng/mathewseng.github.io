**Three biggest wins for this family**
1. **One `ofc/ofc.css` (or `shared/ofc.css`) built on `hub.css` tokens** — page/surface/line/ink/accent `#8fd9b6`, 16/12px radii, Inter features, the hub header (crumbs + switcher with all four OFC pages) — and delete the four local `:root` palettes and ~160 stray hex/rgba literals. The trainer's 3,399-line stylesheet collapses to its "trainer-first" layout once the board, tray, segmented, buttons, dialog and rules article come from the shared file; EV drops its duplicated rules CSS/renderer and the 4,300-line trainer script dependency.
