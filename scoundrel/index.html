How to play

Clear all 44 cards without running out of health. Start at 20 health.

01

Choose three. Leave one.

A room has four encounters. Resolve three in any order, then take the remaining card into the next room. Clear every card in the final room.

♠ ♣

Fight the monsters

Take their full value in damage, or reduce it by your weapon’s power. J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13, A = 14. You can always choose to fight barehanded.

♦

Arm yourself

Diamonds replace your current weapon and reset its chain. After a weapon defeats a monster, it can only fight monsters strictly weaker than that last kill. Barehanded combat preserves the chain.

♥

Drink carefully

Hearts restore health, up to 20. Only the first potion in a room heals you. Additional potions are discarded, even if the first restored nothing.

↗

Know when to leave

Avoid an untouched four-card room to move its cards to the bottom. Choose left to keep their order (1 → 2 → 3 → 4) or right to reverse it (4 → 3 → 2 → 1). Swipe horizontally or use Avoid room to preview both choices. You cannot avoid two rooms in a row, or leave after resolving a card.

Scoring and move analysis

Survive to score your remaining health. Finish at 20 health with a potion as your final card to add its value. On defeat, your score is health after the fatal hit minus all unresolved monster values, including the current room. Move analysis samples unseen cards and simulates future decisions using only revealed information. It remembers the order of cards you returned to the bottom. EVs are estimates of final score, not guaranteed outcomes or proven optimal values.

Weapon chains must strictly decrease; equal monster values are not allowed.
