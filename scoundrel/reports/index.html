Method & rules

No peeking

Analysis receives the visible room, health, equipment, unseen card counts, and the remembered order of returned cards. It never receives the shuffle seed or unseen deck order. Future draws are sampled only when reached.

Future play & pruning

Monte Carlo tree search compares future decisions. A visible-room planner handles healing, weapon chains, and the carried card. Equivalent actions share work, and unlikely contenders receive fewer simulations. A fully known small endgame is solved exactly.

Avoid left or right

Left returns the room in displayed order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4. Right returns it reversed: 4 → 3 → 2 → 1. Both go to the bottom of the deck. You can only avoid an untouched room, and cannot avoid consecutive rooms.

Scoring & combat

Weapon chains strictly decrease. Only the first potion in a room heals. Clear every card in the final room. Survive to score remaining health, with a final-potion bonus at 20 health. Defeat scores include overkill and every unresolved monster.

Seeds, verification, and sources

The fixed seeds use Fisher–Yates shuffles of the 44-card deck. No deals are discarded or replaced. The sample mean estimates this policy’s expected outcome. It is not an exact population EV or a proof of the best possible hidden-deck strategy.

Zach Gage & Kurt Bieg’s original rules leave the order of an avoided room unspecified; this version explicitly offers the two orders above. The PDF’s equal-rank wording is ambiguous; this game and report use strictly decreasing weapon chains.

Engine, solver, generator, and tests on GitHub. The raw results contain every score, the simulation budget, and links to checksum-verified history files.
